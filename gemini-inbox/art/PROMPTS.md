- **Context / Lore Displayed:**
  > *"AYLMER, QUÉBEC — Population 42 000. Fusionné de force avec Gatineau v'là deux ans, mais personne icitte dit qu'y reste à Gatineau. Le chemin Fraser sent le pin et l'asphalte chaud. Fais le plein au Petro-Canada avant d'descendre à la marina."*
- **Visual Subject:** Looking down Rue Principale from the hill near the Catholic church steeple towards the river. A red Civic Si and a white Ranger parked at the curb outside the British Hotel. Summer afternoon heat shimmer over the asphalt.
- **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Cinematic landscape concept art of Rue Principale in Old Aylmer Quebec, summer afternoon in July 2004. Looking down the historic sloped avenue lined with mature green maple trees, two-storey heritage brick shops, and the red brick British Hotel with its wide wooden porch. A white 1993 Ford Ranger pickup and a red late-80s Honda Civic hatchback idling at the stop line. In the far distance down the hill, the glistening blue waters of the Ottawa River. Warm bright sunshine, long soft shadows, nostalgic 2004 Canadian summer town atmosphere, 16:9 ratio.
  ```

  ```text

Towering brutalist concrete architecture of Place du Portage government complex in the background, contrasted with historic red brick worker duplexes in the foreground with wrought-iron spiral staircases. Road construction along Boulevard des Allumettières with bright orange traffic cones and an STO city transit bus passing by. Hazy summer late-afternoon sky with industrial smokestacks along the Ottawa River, 16:9 widescreen painterly concept art.

In the foreground, Island Park Drive active transit path with cyclists, green parkway lawns, Canadian flags fluttering in the breeze.
