# Prototype surface textures

The three colour maps (asphalt, grass, concrete) were shared in Paul’s Toronto Street Circuit project. Its texture credits identify ambientCG assets under CC0 1.0 Universal, downscaled and re-encoded. The source credit list is reproduced below; it does not map every output filename to a specific source ID.
