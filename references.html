AYLMER · ÉTÉ 2004 / ART DIRECTION

A familiar town.
A PC game you could have played in 2004.

Midtown Madness 2’s San Francisco is the reference: textured but simple architecture, broad readable streets, a warm everyday palette, and cars with a little shine. These are reference screenshots, not artwork licensed for use in Aylmer.

What this means for Aylmer

  • Spend the next art budget on recognizable façades, roofs, porches, storefront signs and local roadside clutter.
  • Keep matte asphalt and worn siding. Reserve gentle reflections for cars and water.
  • Use directional sunlight and grounded shadows; preserve readable night driving with blue ambient fill and warm lamps.
  • Keep the existing vehicle handling, geography, characters and campaign. This is a rendering prototype, not a new game.
  • Prioritize Fraser, Principale and the marina as three representative scenes before touching the whole map.

Remote images remain hosted by their sources and require internet access. No Midtown Madness assets are bundled in the game.
