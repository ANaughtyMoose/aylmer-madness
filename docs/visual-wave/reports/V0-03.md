## Hooks (src/game/look/frame.js)

render():
- `look.beforeSky(r, G)` after `r.begin`, before the sky dome. Inits every feature once per renderer.
- `look.afterWorld(r, G)` after world, distant ring and signals, before any car.
- `look.paint(G, spec, opts)` in `drawCar` before the body draws (car paint can add to the body options; no-op today).
- `look.perCar(r, G, spec, x, z, yaw, y, gy)` at the end of `drawCar`: the blob shadow, then any feature exporting `perCar`.
- `look.afterTransparent(r, G)` after the damage fx, before `r.end()`.
- `look.post(r, G)` after `r.end()`, before the HUD.

tick(): `look.tick(dt)` right after `updateDayNight(dt)`.

A module may also export any stage by name (`beforeSky`, `afterWorld`, `afterTransparent`, `post`), or `perCar` / `paint`, and frame.js calls it there. Everything is skipped while its flag is off. Call lists are built once at load; a frame allocates nothing in frame.js.

## Flags (src/game/look/flags.js)

- Every flag tasks.json names, default ON (a stub does nothing whatever its flag says).
- URL: `?look=-ao,-wires` (off), `?look=wires` or `+wires` (on). Read once at load.
- `isOn(flag)`, `set(patch)`, `parse(text)`, `snapshot()`, `defaults()`, `known(name)`, `onChange(fn)`, `PROTOTYPE`.
- Unknown names warn once per name (`console.warn`) and change nothing; `isOn` of an unknown name is false.
- `AYLMER.look({flag: bool})` or `AYLMER.look('-ao,bloom')` sets and returns all flags; `AYLMER.look()` reads.

## AYLMER.time(hour)

- `AYLMER.time(13)` pins the clock at 13:00 in both entries and applies the sky at once; it holds through steps (no midnight passes in the 2004 calendar, and it overrides a mission's own time of day while pinned). `AYLMER.time(null)` lets the clock run again; `AYLMER.time()` reads the hour.
- The 2004 clock is the hour. The classic cycle has no hours, so an hour is placed in its four phases: morning from 06:00, day 09:30, dusk 19:00, night 21:30 to 06:00. 07:00, 13:00, 19:30 and 23:30 all land inside the phase holds, so they are exactly `TIME_OF_DAY.morning/day/dusk/night`.
- `AYLMER.weather(name)`, `AYLMER.env(name)` and `AYLMER.visualClock(hour)` are unchanged.
