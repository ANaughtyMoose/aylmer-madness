# Aylmer Madness script, draft v0

21 September 2026. Claude, story editor. NOTHING HERE IS IN THE GAME. Every line is a PROPOSAL unless marked KEEP (existing line retained) or USER CONFIRMED. Routes, objectives, rewards and mechanics are untouched.

## Rules this draft follows

1. **Settled 22 Sept 2026: French narrator, English bubbles.** Briefs, stage directions, hints, toasts and HUD stay Québécois French. Every spoken bubble is English, Margaret included. She calls him Tom.
2. Bubbles are 12 words or fewer, one per beat, never two in a row from the same person unless one is a reaction to the other.
3. Mike's lines are drafted in English with the rest; his existing French lines are kept where they are already good, marked KEEP.
4. Rudy is named once, in the opener, with no claim about him. Nobody's substance history appears. Still cuttable.
5. The gas-money gag is planted straight in job 1 and escalates in the completion toasts only.
6. Margaret is never a passenger with the gang. See STORY-LEDGER.md section 5a for the six missions still being untangled.

Format: **Brief** (French, on the job card) / **Start** (bubbles at accept) / **On the road** (toasts and mid-drive bubbles at existing stage points) / **End** (bubbles at completion) / **Toast** (the completion line).
