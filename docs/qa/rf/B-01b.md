# B-01b Never stranded: job-start swap or refusal on every job

Branch rf/b-01b, from fix/look-on-qr 9d19d9e. Shift B1, merge 4 of 8.

## What changed

- `src/game/jobready.js` (owned): new `ensureOwnVehicle(mission, G, opts)`, plus
  `jobKind`, `bringSpot`, `swapLine`, `refuseLine`, `SELF_VEHICLE`, `KEEP_NEAR` (40 m),
  `HOME_NEAR` (60 m). A job is checked when it is `race` (def.race), `timed` (any stage
  with a `time`) or `cargo` (passengers, or a load / lift / fragile / slidingbox / bag
  stage). `golfcart` and `canot` bring their own vehicle and are never swapped.
  The vehicle is checked with garage.js `vehicleRole`: `own` and `lent` (a friend's car
  the garage has handed you) pass; `cart`, `bike` (two wheels and not yours) and
  `loaner` (anything the garage does not give you, or flagged `loaner`) do not.
  On a miss: your own vehicle parked within 40 m is used where it stands; otherwise it
  is brought to the pillar, 8 m ahead of you on a checked road spot (tow.js
  `placementReason` / `roadSpot`), or to the nose-in spot at 299 Fraser when the job
  starts within 60 m of home (homeparking.js `fraserParking`, hard rule 4). The swap is
  free and is the start key, so it is one key. If nothing can be placed (or
  `opts.bring === false` and the car is far) it refuses with a narrator line and a GPS
  point at your own vehicle.
  - Swap line: « Ton Ranger t’attend au pilier, la clé dessus. Le cart reste ici. Gratis. »
  - Refusal: « Pas en cart pour cette job-là. Va chercher ton Ranger, le GPS te montre le chemin. »
  The vehicle name follows the character (Ta Civic, Ton vélo, Ton Forester, Ta Sienna).
- `src/game/garage.js` (owned): `ownCarId(character)` (save.js CHARACTERS[].car),
  `vehicleRole(spec, { character, garage, veh })` and `handBack(G, { spot })`, the
  garage hand-back: whatever you are in stays where you got out (the game's own
  `G.swapCar`) and you are put in your own vehicle, brought to `spot` if one is given.
- `src/game/setpieces/royal.js` (owned): Russell's pickup gate now uses the shared test
  (`strandedIn(G)`, exported): cart, any bike (Russell needs a seat, even on Zahra's own
  bike) or a loaner. Same line as before. It is the backstop if a start skips
  `ensureOwnVehicle`.
- `tools/smoke_jobready.mjs` (owned): sections 8 and 9 (B-01b). Sections 1 to 7 untouched.
- `tools/review/beats/B-01b.mjs` (owned, new): script beat that stands in for the
  startMission call in the real page (see below).

No game-visible placement in the world (the brought car is put next to the player),
so public-place.mjs does not apply. No main-cast line was written.

## CONTRACT NOTES (for B-12a, src/main.js)

1. In `startMission(def, opts)`, right after the `missionAvailable` check and before
   `cancelRetry` / `const spec = G.veh.spec` / `def.build(...)`, call
   `const own = jobHooks.ensureOwnVehicle ? jobHooks.ensureOwnVehicle(def, G) : null;`
   (load it on the same lazy `import('./game/jobready.js')` that sets
   `jobHooks.prepareForJob`: add `jobHooks.ensureOwnVehicle = m.ensureOwnVehicle || null`).
2. If `own && own.action === 'refuse'`: `hud.toast(own.line, 3200); if (own.gps) G.waypoint = own.gps; return false;`
   If `own && own.action === 'swap'`: `hud.toast(own.line, 3000);` and carry on (G.veh is
   now the own vehicle, so `def.build` gets the right seats). `'ok'` changes nothing.
3. Garage hand-back: wherever main.js hands a borrowed vehicle back (the garage menu,
   the end of a set piece that lent you one), call `handBack(G)` from
   `src/game/garage.js` (`handBack(G, { spot: bringSpot(G, ownCarId(G.character)) })`
   to bring the car when it is far). It returns `{ ok, swapped, from, to, why }`.
   Signatures: `ensureOwnVehicle(mission, G, opts = {})` (mission = def or G.mission
   record), `handBack(G, { spot } = {})`, `vehicleRole(spec, opts)`, `ownCarId(character)`.

B-01a exports `returnToOwnVehicle(reason)` from tow.js for Y; both can share
`garage.handBack`, and if they disagree the owner (B-12a) wins.
