## What changed

- **src/game/style.js (new).** `StyleChain` and the module instance `style`. Four events feed one chain x1 to x3:
  traffic near misses (gap <= 1.5 m, closing > 50 km/h, traffic.js's event shape), metres in the oncoming lane at >= 50 km/h
  (a moving car within 45 m ahead, in your lane, coming at you; one paid event per 50 m, and being there keeps the chain alive),
  handbrake slides (skid >= 0.35 at >= 25 km/h, paid at the end if >= 0.8 s) and flights >= `AIR.comboMin` (1.0 s).
  Ceiling `AIR.comboMax` (3) and decay `AIR.comboWindow / 2` (3 s), both imported from jumps.js. Steps: 1-2 events x1, 3-5 x2, 6+ x3.
  Table `PAY = { near: 4, oncoming: 3, slide: 4 (+1 $ a second past the first, at most +2), air: 3 }` times the multiplier.
  Toast requests read `+12 $ FRÔLÉ x3`, `+9 $ CONTRESENS x3`, `+15 $ DÉRAPAGE x3`, `+9 $ DANS LES AIRS x3`, without the dollar part when not paying.
  x3 held 10 s asks for one friend line per chain (cooldown 60 s), only from `FRIEND_LINES`. Right now that is two of Sayyad's existing English golf-night lines,
  checked against `speakerLines('Sayyad','en')`. No new cast line was written.
  Exports: `style.tick(dt, events, opts)`, `style.sense(dt, v, nearList, cars, handbrake)`, `style.hudState()`, `style.statsInto(stats)`, `friendLine`, `allFriendLines`.
- **src/game/reactive.js.** It now holds the chain and runs it every drive tick (`styleStep`, after traffic.update, from the same `G.reactive.update(dt, G)` call main.js already makes).
  It reads `traffic.nearMisses`, the per-step list, so `drainNearMisses()` stays free for B-09 or B-12a. It pays tips straight to `G.wallet`, keeps `G.mission.styleTips` (never `m.paid`, so a failed job does not claw tips back),
  toasts through `hud.styleTip(text, ms)` if hud.js has it and `hud.toast` if not. Toasts are gated by `missionNotificationAllowed(G,'stunt')`, so golf night stays quiet.
  It counts qualifying traffic near misses into `G.stats.nearMiss`, says the friend line with `heckle.line(who, line, 3000, { group: 'friend' })`,
  sets `G.style`, and writes the style keys to G.stats: `styleTips`, `styleNear`, `styleSlides`, `styleAir`, `styleOncomingM` and `styleBestChain`.
  The job panel (#reactstats) gets a `STYLE n $  xM` line.
- **src/game/missionkit.js.** `missionStyleBonus`: once the chain is live (`now.styleTips` defined), the end of the job pays only the clean-car 5 $ and reports `n $ de style en route`.
  The 8 / 9 / 5 caps and the 22 $ ceiling no longer bind, because near misses and jumps pay live and uncapped. A caller with bare stats keeps the old capped sum, so nothing pays twice (tools/smoke.mjs and smoke_econ unchanged and green).
  New `styleTipsPaying(G)`: true only on a job's first run, never on a replay, an M-menu course or in free roam. `STYLE_END` holds the numbers.
- **src/game/economy.js.** Unchanged (owned, not needed).
- **tools/smoke_style.mjs (new), tools/review/beats/B-03.mjs (new).**
