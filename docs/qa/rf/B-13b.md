## 1. The 683 ms hitch

**What the packs said.** In `20261013-002218-full` the worst stvincent frame
(683 ms) started 0.36 s after stage 1 (the heat drive) began, with 300, 183,
167 and 117 ms frames later in the drive; `052835-ranger-rerun` had 517 ms
0.03 s after stage 1 began. Both were in the golf cart, after golfcart and
arcsurchauffe, deep into a long session. `065209-ranger-rerun` had none at the
stage 1 start (267 ms at 135 s into the fresh column's drive).

**What reproduced.** `tools/review/beats/B-13b.mjs` replays that moment
without the 13 km drive: seed the job, stand on Sayyad's kerb, hold E for the
plank, time every rAF gap to 6 s into stage 1, then force the cab heat to 0.1,
0.6 and 1 (blur, glow, wobble on) and time rolling frames and a harness
screenshot at each. Results, Ranger then cart, each a cold browser:

| Run | transition max | hot drive max |
|---|---|---|
| probe-before (first run, machine busy) | 16.8 ms | **2616.6 ms** (950, 416, 1050, 300 on the way) |
| same run, cart pass | 16.8 | 16.8 |
| prof1, prof2 (cart cold) | 16.8 | 16.8 |
| probe-old-1..3, 6 passes | 16.8 | 16.8 |

Then an A/B of the screen effect alone in free roam (`b13b-lab3/4/5`): the
original effect never went over 33.4 ms in 6 passes; a rewrite that moved the
effect off the canvas (plain alpha layers, compositor-only shimmer, blur in
0.5 px steps) spiked 783, 166 and 750 ms in 3 of 6 passes. So the original
CSS filter chain is not the hitch on its own, and the rewrite was worse:
**it was dropped and `sauna_heat.js` stays as it was**.

**What the hitch is, as far as the evidence goes.** It does not come back in
a short session with the same code, the same stage, the same vehicle and the
same screenshots. It comes with session age and machine load. The bot's own
work at that moment is small (one 13 km `nav.route`, 8 to 17 ms in node).
What could not be shown here without a long trace is the frame itself, so the
bot now names every long frame (below): the next full pack says whether a
stvincent frame over 200 ms is script (whose function, which file), style and
layout, or nothing the page ran (GPU, compositor, screenshot, collector).
The first packs with it already name the boot hitches: 403 ms
`BUTTON#start.onclick` (main.js), 907 ms `FrameRequestCallback` (ui.js),
522 ms `Worker.onmessage` (world/sector-client.js), 934 ms
`Response.json.then` (game/signage.js).

**Result.** In-task: harness stvincent max raw frame **83.3 ms**
(`b13b-after-1`, Ranger, whole beat, both columns) and **116.7 ms** in the
cart context (`b13b-after-chain`, golfcart then arcsurchauffe then stvincent,
the conditions of the 683 ms frame), against the 250 ms bar (was 683).
The second Ranger run (after-2) is in section 4: 116.7 ms.

## 2. The 5 teleports

Read from the traces of the three packs (`traces/stvincent.csv`):

| Stage | Teleports | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| 0, Sayyad's kerb (1.7 km) | 2 | The bot was in the golf cart (24 km/h). The stage's time limit assumed 62 km/h, so it ran out at 251 s, 40 % short. The teleport then put it 32 m from the target *on the straight line toward the truck*: a yard. Stuck 23 m out, four stuck re-plans, second teleport onto the target. |
| 1, the drive to St. Vincent (13 km) | 1 | The watchdog counted "12 s without getting 15 m closer" as the crow flies. The route starts by driving north 200 m while the hospital is 10 km east, so every 12 s was a "failed re-plan" for a bot driving fine; four of them (plus one real stop) and it was teleported at 67 s. |
| 2, the hospital lot (r 22) | 2 | The stage 1 teleport landed 40 m from the kerb point on the straight line to Aylmer: behind the hospital, off the road. Stuck at 27 m, a second teleport to the same bad spot, a third onto the target. |
| 3, the meal service | abandon | The bot walked at random with E for 480 s and never reached a bed. |

All four are the bot's, none is the set piece's. Fixes in `page-agent.js`:

- **Progress along the route**: the watchdog measures the remaining route
  length (`restLengths`, distance to the next point plus the rest of the
  polyline), not the straight line.
- **Assists land on the road**: `assistSpot(tx, tz, off)` walks `off` metres
  back from the target along the `nav.route` to it and faces down the route.
- **Time limit in the vehicle driven**: the careful-driver speed is capped by
  `spec.topSpeed` (0.9 x), and the limit by `LEG_CAP_S = 420` s so a long leg
  still gets its assist inside the harness's 900 s per job.
- **The heat, read like the prompt says**: F when the cab is at 85 % with water
  in the jug; a detour to the nearest dépanneur when the jug is dry and one is
  within 2.5 km, or when one is within 220 m and the jug is not full; stop, E.
  `sauna.js` exposes the nearest refill as `G.sauna.refill` (read-only).
- **The meal service, played**: walk to the next bed still waiting, wait for
  its green, E (the game takes E as Space). 9 bites in 14.9 s.
- Each assist now records `why` (`time` or `replans`) and `onRoute`.

**Result.** In-task, harness stvincent teleports: **0** in the Ranger
(`b13b-after-1`), **1** in the cart context (`b13b-after-chain`: stage 1, a
real stuck on a corner at 24 km/h, `why: replans`, `onRoute: true`), against
the bar of 1 (was 5). In the cart context the job is **done** for the first
time in any pack: stages 362.7 s, 79.5 s, 7 s, 14.9 s.

## 3. The smoke_react flake

The only timing check is P8 "streetprops bake < 60 ms". Quiet, the bake is
15 ms. During this task the machine was loaded enough that the world bake
before it took 47-55 s instead of 9 s, and best-of-5 wall time still failed
once (64 ms). It is now checked on the **CPU time of the baking thread**
(`process.threadCpuUsage()`), best of up to 5 bakes on fresh stub renderers,
retried only while the best is over half the budget. **Same 60 ms budget**;
first-bake and rebake wall times are printed beside it. Loaded run: 27 ms CPU
against 62 ms wall.

In-task: smoke_react passed **5 of 5** full `npm test` runs on the final code (runs 4 to 8 in a row, all 95/95), under load.
