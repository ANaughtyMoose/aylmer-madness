# B-07 Fair pay and fair fails: escape pays, story fails keep the day

Branch rf/b-07 from fix/look-on-qr 9d19d9e. Shift B1, merge 5 of 8.

## What changed

- **src/game/cops.js**: `ESCAPE_PAY` (1 star 20 $, 2 stars 60 $, 3 stars 180 $) and `escapePay(stars)`. `Cops` tracks `peak` (most stars this chase reached, reset by `reset()`, `clear()` and so by a bust). When the heat bleeds to zero after a real chase, the new `escaped(G)` pays `escapePay(peak)` through `G.wallet.add` (money.js's existing API, money.js untouched), puts the amount on the « Tu les as semés. +N $ » toast, and leaves `{ stars, pay }` on `cops.lastEscape`. A scripted escape stage (verbs.js `loseThem`, stage kind `escape`) pays 0 here, because its job pays it. Not scaled by difficulty: the plan's table is exact.
- **src/game/retry.js**: `storyFail(m)` (campaign.js MAIN ids plus the finale) and `failSpendsDay(m)` (never for the story, else missionkit's `failCostsDay`). `offerRetry` marks a story job's day as kept (`calendar.keepDay`) before main.js's `endOfJob()` runs, never defers a story day, and puts `story: true` on the offer; a second story fail shows « R : on recommence l’étape ({s} s). La journée reste. » (new fallback `card.story`, HUD French) instead of « Ça coûte la journée ». The fail toast state now lives here: `setFailToast`, `failToast`, `tickFailToast`, `clearFailToast`. `offerRetry` sets it, `tickRetry` runs its clock, `takeRetry` and `cancelRetry` (every startMission) clear it.
- **src/game/calendar.js**: `keepDay(G, id)` and `unkeepDay(G)`. `spendDay` consumes the mark once and spends nothing only while that same job is still `G.mission` (main.js's failMission spends before closeMission), so a stale mark can never eat another job's day.
- **tools/smoke_retry.mjs**: the A-03 deferred-day flow now runs on a paid job (blitz), since the story no longer has a day to defer; three new B-07 groups below. No check was loosened: every A-03 check still runs, on a job where the rule still applies.
