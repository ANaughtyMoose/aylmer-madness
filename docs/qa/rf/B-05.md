## What changed

### Sayyad's doughnuts (stunts.js, sidejobs.js, props.js)

- **Half-turns count.** DoughnutMeter now banks every half-turn (PI) as it is done. Two banked halves are one doughnut. A broken slide or a change of direction only loses the part of the current half-turn not yet banked. The prompt shows a banked half as « ½ » (« Doughnuts: 1½/3 ») and a short blip plays on each half.
- **A break under 1.2 s keeps the lap.** DONUT.pause 1.0 -> 1.2 s.
- **Doughnuts from rest.** Root cause, measured headless (cars.js on tarmac): with W, full lock and the lever held from a stop, the lever's drag (spec.brake x 0.55 = 4.4 m/s2 on the Ranger) beats the engine (3.5 m/s2), so the truck tops out at 1.6 km/h, under the meter's 8 km/h. New `doughnutAssist(v, ctl, dt)` in stunts.js: inside the doughnut zone, only with the lever, the gas (>= 0.5) and the wheel (>= 0.3) all held, not in reverse, in the air or on two wheels, it cancels the lever drag and adds 1.5 m/s2 of drive up to 20 km/h. The rear then slides round. The Sayyad stage calls it each tick after the vehicle update (main.js runs v.update, then updateMission).
- **What counts as sliding.** The Ranger's lever slide at full lock runs about 2.2 m/s sideways and 0.84 rad/s of model yaw (the rest of the rotation is the A-02 handbrake swing), so the old thresholds (3.0 m/s, 1.2 rad/s) never saw it. Now slipLat 1.8 m/s, slipYawRate 0.6 rad/s (the Sienna slides at 0.64). minKmh stays 8; zone radius stays 22 m.
- **Chalk ring.** `chalkring` mesh (28 unlit dashes, alpha 0.55, radius 7.5 m) placed on PLACES.sayyad, the point resolvePlaces() already snapped onto the street in front of the 75. It sits on the road and the strip of lawn at the curb. The hint and sub now say « dans le cercle de craie ». Note: the plan says "on the lawn"; I centred it on the street spot the meter already counts from, because the stage hint, the bot and the damage target all point at the street, and the house is 10 to 15 m behind the curb.
- **Live progress arc.** `donutarc1..16` meshes (16 tiny meshes uploaded once at load; filling the arc is a mesh swap, never a rebuild), radius 3.9 m round the truck, alpha 0.8, unlit. `donutArcFollow()` anchors the arc where the current doughnut started, so its leading end is always the nose. Hidden before the first slide and after the third doughnut. The ring and the arc go away on stage exit (onExit) and with the job (`job:` prefix).

### The getaway (sidejobs.js)

- Found by the first harness run: with the doughnuts fixed (18.9 s, was abandoned at 225 s), the job then failed at « Sacre ton camp! 300 m en 25 s ». That stage had `noTarget` and `noRoute`: no marker, no GPS line, so a player who does not know the street guesses a direction, and the review bot (which drives to markers) sat still for 25 s.
- `escapeSpot(G)`: a short Dijkstra on G.nav (road length) from Sayyad's street to the first road node 340 m out in a straight line (ESCAPE_M + 40). It is at (-707.8, -805.8): 341 m straight, 360 m of road. The stage now has `at: escapeSpot, radius 25, anywhere: true`, so the marker and the GPS line show the quickest way out, while the stage still passes anywhere past 300 m. Sub and hint say so. public-place on the marker: pass, 355.2 m from the nearest private home. Computed once per session (0.7 ms).

### Le canot (sidejobs.js)

- **Root cause of the dead air.** During the 386 s paddle in pack 20261013-002218-full, the compass prompt (water %, distance, arrows) was set from onTick, and stageStep then overwrote it with the stage hint six seconds in (HINT_AFTER) every tick. The player lost the compass and the transcript saw one frozen prompt. Between the three leak lines nothing else was said: gaps of 113, 102 and 96 s.
- **Fix 1:** the compass is now the paddle stage's own `prompt()` (`paddleCompass(b)`). The « Psst » hint still shows, but only while the canoe sits still (|vLong| < 0.3 m/s).
- **Fix 2:** `paddleBeat()`: whenever the crossing has gone PADDLE_QUIET (30 s) without a line (leak lines reset it), the next of 12 narrator lines comes up. Every line is an existing narrator line, word for word, from the marina and beach pools of assets/text/ambient.json; smoke_doughnut checks each one is still there. No new line, no cast line. The haul was not shortened: the island prop is placed by main.js and PLACES.island lives in places.js, neither owned.
