# A-06 Main loop feel: speed cues, crash and jump slow-mo, honest cards, T gated

## What changed

- **Speed FOV (src/main.js render).** The old ramp (0.16 rad from 8 to 42 m/s) stays; a second eased ramp adds 0.26 rad from 70 to 130 km/h. At 100 km/h the total is about 0.22-0.24 rad (was about 0.09). `G.feel.speedFov` holds the last value.
- **Speed shake.** A light two-sine pitch and yaw sway above 70 km/h, scaled by the shake setting, off in the air.
- **Wind and roar (src/core/audio.js `wind(kmh)`).** One looping noise voice, two bands (a 1.2-2.1 kHz hiss, a 140-260 Hz roar), smoothstep from 70 to 130 km/h. `audio.meters` holds `{wind, windGain, roarGain}`. Silenced wherever the engine is silenced (pause, cinematic hold, menu).
- **Slow-mo and camera punch.** `fireSlowmo('crash')` when an impact rises above 0.6 force with the car above 40 km/h just before; `fireSlowmo('jump')` when a flight passes 1.6 s, while still in the air. Crash 0.4 s at 0.3x, jump 0.5 s at 0.35x, eased back over the last third, 2.5 s cooldown. frame() stretches sim time only; `AYLMER.step()` never stretches its dt (harness step counts unchanged), it only counts the slow-mo down. The punch narrows the FOV by up to 0.12 rad and springs back. `AYLMER.feel()` returns the tallies.
- **Honest card footer.** `cardNote()` names the rival(s) on a race: "Course contre Adam. Pas de chrono : arrive le premier." (EN: "Race against Adam. No clock: get there first."). Timed stages still list their clocks. Needs `def.rivals`, a one-line addition to `raceMission()` in src/game/racejobs.js (not an owned file; nobody owns it this wave).
- **T gated.** `doReset()` does nothing unless `towUI.stuck` (the tow.js stuck watch). The jerrycan on T is unchanged.
- **A-03 wired** to its contract: `offerRetry` + `retryCard` in failMission, `failCostsDay` and no endOfJob on a deferred day, `tickRetry` before the radio reads KeyR, `cancelRetry` in startMission, loadIntoGame and toMenu, `loadFails()` at boot. This branch merges ra/A-03 (44450e6) so retry.js is a static import.
- **A-01 hook.** `prepareForJob(G.veh, G.mission, G)` is called at the end of startMission through a lazy import, because ra/A-01 had not committed when this was built. It is idempotent, so it is harmless next to the static call A-01 is adding to main.js itself.
