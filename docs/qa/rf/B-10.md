## What changed

- **src/game/cinematic.js.** New exports `CARD_GRADES` (day, dusk, night, rain), `CARD_WEATHER` (`{ golfcart: 'rain' }`), `cardGrade(def)`, `cardArt(def)`, `resolveArt(art)`, `gradeStyle(grade, src)`. A grade is CSS only, set inline on the `.cinema-art` layer once the painting has loaded: colour layers (linear and radial gradients, and for rain a tiled streak pattern) over the shared painting with `background-blend-mode`, then a `filter` chain. No image is baked from fraser-2004.png, so E-05's new home picture needs no rebuild. `show()` now takes `job` (a mission def): with no `art`, the art is `cardArt(job)`. Art keys are `home`, `alternateur`, or `home@<grade>`; `root.dataset.art` stays the painting (`home`), so cinematic.css's `left center` crop still applies, and `root.dataset.grade` names the grade. `hide()` clears the inline grade. Stale loads are still dropped (`this.request === request`).
- Mapping: the job's own `timeOfDay` (morning and day give the painting as painted, dusk gives dusk, night gives night); `CARD_WEATHER` wins (golfcart is rain: a card-art choice from the plan, not a story claim, since no def has a weather field). alternateur keeps its own painting ungraded. Side and race jobs get the grade of their hour too.
- **assets/cinema/tints/rain.svg** (5.5 KB, seeded script, no source image) and a README.
- **tools/smoke_cinematic.mjs.** Every MAIN + FINALE id maps to its expected card (table `EXPECT`); golfcart is rain, nuittim night; fallbacks; every grade's CSS is well formed. Then the grades applied in node to the painting's pixels, on the crop the card shows at 1280x800, with the same numbers the page uses. Then `show()` on a stub: job picks the grade, late load dropped, hide clears, explicit art wins, own paintings ungraded.
- **tools/review/beats/B-10.mjs.** In the live page, for every main card: open it as the game does today and with `show({ job })`, capture the art box as PNG, measure. PNGs by absolute path to `aylmer-madness/references/review/rf-b10/cards/{before,after}/` for look-diff. Plan file (private): `references/review/plans-rf/B-10.json` (boot, then the beat).
