## What changed

### Major 1: style tips no longer flood the one HUD toast slot
- reactive.js `styleStep` sends tips only to `hud.styleTip(text, ms)`, B-06's chain slot under the « STYLE xN » readout (it shows the frame it is called). It never calls `hud.toast` for the chain. A HUD without `styleTip` gets nothing.
- One tip line per step: two events in the same step are coalesced (`tipText`: « +21 $ FRÔLÉ + CONTRESENS x3 »).
- A step that pays nothing (a replay, a course) shows no tip line; the readout still shows the chain.
- B-03.md contract note 3 was wrong (it said two toast slots and called styleTip optional): the HUD runs one slot with a 2.5 s floor, so `hud.toast` is unsafe for the chain. The `G.input` sentence was also wrong: G.input is already set and the real handbrake already gates slides.

### Major 2: threading traffic draws no police heat
- Traffic near misses are no longer added to `G.stats.nearMiss`. They are counted only on the chain (`G.stats.styleNear`). `G.stats.nearMiss` stays what it was before B-03 (pedestrian grazes), which is what cops.js:276 reads for « de la conduite dangereuse » and jumps.js reads for « frôlé en l'air ». No cops.js change.
- Display only: the job panel's FRÔLÉS shows nearMiss + styleNear, and the FINI card's feats line counts both, so the player still sees every near miss counted.

### Major 3: oncoming metres pay in real driving
- Before: metres counted only on frames with a car 3 to 45 m ahead within 2.0 m laterally, and reset to 0 on any other frame, so one car (about 1.26 s in the window, 24.5 m of travel) never reached the 50 m pay step. The 40 to 90 $ acceptance rested on smoke_style writing `c._onM` directly.
- Now (style.js `laneSide` + `sense`): where you are comes from traffic.js's real road graph, looked up every 0.1 s: you are on the wrong side when you are at least 0.6 m left of the centreline of a two-way street (and still on it), or going against a one-way. The metres count while you are there at 50 km/h or more AND an oncoming car is within 90 m ahead (3.5 m either side of your line), or one passed you under 1.5 s ago. An empty wrong side pays nothing. A dodge back to the line to pass a car keeps the metres for 2 s. One paid event every 40 m (was 50).
- Measured in node through sense() with a moving car: one car at 50 km/h met on its side at 70 km/h pays 82 m, 2 events (before: 0). On the centreline (the bot's line) and in your own lane: 0 m.
- traffic.js buckets edges by midpoint, so a long straight can sit outside the 3 x 3 cells around you: edges longer than 170 m are kept in one list per graph (built on the first drive frame, about 8 ms in node, once) and always checked. Lookup about 10 µs, every 0.1 s.

### Not a review item: free roam pays (lead to confirm)
- `styleTipsPaying` now pays in free roam (`STYLE_FREE_ROAM = true` in missionkit.js, the one switch). Before, free roam showed the chain and paid 0 $, which fails Tom's bar (driving well must visibly pay) and the task's ask for pay in metrics.json on speedRun and cops, both free-roam beats. Replays and M-menu courses still pay nothing. See "For the lead" below.
