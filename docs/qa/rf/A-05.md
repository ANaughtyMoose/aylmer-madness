## Implemented under initial ownership

- `camera-colliders.js`: CPU copies of actual rendered position/index ranges, indexed by per-building AABBs in a 32 m grid. Finite two-sided triangle sweeps test faces, edge capsules and vertex spheres continuously. Initial overlap may leave a surface but may not enter it. Final eye overlap is checked separately.
- `camclip.js`: immediate inward correction, exponential outward release, post-smoothing guard, alternate visible-side booms when a crash leaves no usable room behind the vehicle. No minimum boom fraction can force the camera into geometry. Ground lifts are rechecked.
- Final-render statistics separately report ray obstruction, eye overlap, swept-sphere obstruction, missing source geometry and focus framing from the actual view-projection matrix. Missing geometry is unknown, never a successful sample.
- `tools/qa/camera.mjs` and `camera-poses.json`: explicit reproducible stress poses at the locations associated with old shots 174 and 270, with before/after image and actual-eye metadata. Old yaw and eye metadata do not exist. The new fixture records this limitation rather than claiming reconstruction.

## Known limits and follow-up contracts

- Close stress views crop the vehicle, and `framed` checks the focus point rather than its entire bounding box. The moving evidence above verifies useful side views and subsequent full framing for the tested sequence.
- Camera collision does not change vehicle physics. The mall and crash images still show vehicle overlap with rendered signs/posts or porch geometry; those existing physics mismatches are not cured by a clear camera ray.
- Standard look-diff has inherited asphalt-palette misses, and enlarged white-truck highlights remain a Wave B grading issue. The custom look-diff pass must not be reported as lighting acceptance.
- No whole-game performance claim follows from camera CPU timings. FreeRoam's rolling frame statistics include a 383.4 ms maximum, and the existing long-frame filtering limits those statistics. Hitch and frame-evidence work remains with A-10/A-11; budgets were not raised.
- Packed geometry bytes exclude JS grid, block and string overhead. No total-heap or isolated source-capture-overhead measurement is claimed.
- Worker integration must preserve every position/index typed buffer and triangle order from `cameraColliders.toPacket()`, then rebuild query closures with `makeCameraColliders(packet)`. Sector residency must continue to drive `cameraReady`; unloaded geometry is unknown. Future opaque surface emitters must capture their final finite mesh ranges, preserving real openings and excluding foliage/gravel.
