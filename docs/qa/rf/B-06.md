## What changed

src/game/hud.js
- **Sub line fades.** `setObjective` clocks the sub line when its text changes (the same line set again keeps its clock, so free roam's 0.3 s refresh does not restart it). It gets `.faded` 5 s later (`SUB_FADE_MS`), or as soon as the tutorial ticks a chip after the line went up. The fade is a timer, not a frame count, so a slow or missing frame cannot keep it up. `pulseObjective` (the stuck nudge) brings it back for another 5 s.
- **Tab chip.** `tickSlots` puts `.hud-gone` on `#tuto` while its cap is « Tab » and `G.playtime >= 120` (`TAB_CHIP_S`). `centreShows` ignores a gone layer. The pause screen keyboard still lists Tab.
- **Style chain readout** `#stylechain` (bottom right, above the stars): `setStyle(hs)` renders style.js `hudState()` (« STYLE x3 », a decay bar from `fade`, orange at x3). `styleTip(text, ms)` is the B-03 contract: reactive.js already calls `hud.styleTip` when it exists, so tips leave the one toast slot (this answers B-03's review major 1 on the HUD side) and show under the readout.
- **Wrong-vehicle chip** `#vehchip` (bottom left, above the job counters): in the cart or on a bike, off a job, « En cart. Y ramène ton Ranger (1,8 km). »; at a timed, race or cargo pillar (nearest job within 40 m, `jobready.jobKind`), in red: « Pas en cart pour une job chronométrée. Y ramène ton Ranger. ». A friend's car gets the chip only at such a pillar. Narrator French, no dashes.
- **Own-truck minimap marker:** a pale yellow square where `tow.ownVehicleStatus(G).at` is, on the rim with its distance when off the map, whenever you are not in your own vehicle.
- **Fail toast:** `tickSlots` also passes retry.js `failToast()` to the existing `failCard` (same source main.js already passes; idempotent).
- All slots are driven by `tickSlots()`, called from `setSpeed()` (once a frame) and doing work at most every 100 ms (`SLOT_MS`). They read `window.AYLMER.G` the way `peekMission()` already does. Pure helpers exported for tests: `subFaded`, `tabChipGone`, `vehicleChip`, `borrowedRole`, `chainView`; `slotState()` for checks.

style.css
- Objective on one line (ellipsis). `#sub.faded` opacity 0 (0.35 s). The clock gets a plate (`#timer` background, dimmer when pending).
- **Doubled FRÔLÉS digits:** cause found. reactive.js builds a new `#reactstats` on every sector seam (main.js `refreshReactive`) and never removes the old one, which stays up with stale counts under the new one (shot 858: « FRÔLÉS 24 » over « 23 »). Fixed in CSS: `#reactstats:has(~ #reactstats){display:none !important}`, so only the newest shows. The root fix (remove the old element) belongs to reactive.js's owner.
- Styles for `#stylechain`, `#vehchip`, `.hud-gone`; both slots hidden under `body.modal`.

src/game/story.js
- 268: the "all done" sub line no longer says « Tab pour la carte ».
- 350 (stvincent start): Tom's French line is replaced by a narrator line, « Tom stationne le Ranger pis rentre servir les repas. » (who: `Narrateur`, tagged walkon by smoke_lang's speaker rule, French either way; smoke_story requires a non-empty speaker).

assets/text/story.proposed.json (new): Tom's English version for Tom's batch (rule 9): « I park the Ranger, then I go in for the meals. »

tools/qa/hud-overlap.mjs: unit checks for the new rules and a node cost bench; live boxes for `#stylechain`, `#vehchip` and every `#reactstats`; top-left text lines once the sub is 5 s old (at most 2); Tab chip after 2 min (0 frames).

tools/review/beats/B-06.mjs (new): top-left after 5.6 s on alternateur and sayyad, Tab chip at 30 s and 130 s, chain readout latency, cart at a timed pillar, overlaps.

## Per-frame cost (cap 0.10 ms)

`tickSlots()` is called once a frame from `setSpeed()` and returns at once unless 100 ms have passed. The work it then does: one getElementById-free class check on #tuto, the sub class, `failToast()`, `G.style.hudState()` and `ownVehicleStatus(G)`; only in a borrowed vehicle off a job, `nearestJob` + `jobKind` (cached per def). DOM is written only when a string changes. The minimap marker is two fillRects when you are out of your truck. Measured in node (hud-overlap unit, worst case: the cart near pillars): **40 µs per slot read, 0.0067 ms per frame at 60 fps** (loaded machine). The binding number is the integration owner's quiet-window perf8 run.

## CONTRACT NOTES (integration owner, B-12b)

1. **No main.js call is needed for any B-06 slot.** `hud.setSpeed()` (already called once a frame in render) ticks them through `Hud.tickSlots()`. They read `window.AYLMER.G` (as `peekMission()` does): `G.style.hudState()` (B-03 sets `G.style`), `tow.ownVehicleStatus(G)`, `retry.failToast()`, `jobready.jobKind`, `story.nearestJob`.
2. **B-03:** reactive.js already sends tips to `hud.styleTip(text, ms)` when it exists, so once B-03 merges its tips leave the toast slot with no further wiring. Do not also call `hud.toast` for style tips. `hud.setStyle(hs)` is public if main.js ever wants to push the state itself.
3. Keep `hud.failCard(failToast())` in frame() as it is; hud.js also polls it (same source, idempotent). `hud.slotState()` returns `{ chain, vehChip, own, subFaded, tabGone }` for After-integration checks; metric keys from the beat: `metrics.json beats[B-06].result.{topLeft,tab,chain,cart}`.
