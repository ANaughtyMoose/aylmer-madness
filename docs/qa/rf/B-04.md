## What changed

- **La rampe de garnotte is on the GPS line** (src/game/jumps.js). It sat on the
  south verge of chemin d'Aylmer at (648.6, -246.3), 14.0 m off the alternateur
  route, and the bot never hit it. It is now a gravel hump right across chemin
  d'Aylmer, lip at (806.0, -248.5), yaw -1.4677 (west, toward the Galeries):
  0.01 m from the route polyline, 452 m of road from the driveway, 63 m past
  the corner off Fraser.
  - Why not further west: any ramp between x 655 and 802 on this road sits
    within 70 m (jumps.js SITE_D) of smoke_jumps' two-minute washboard drive,
    which must stay "a long way from any ramp". A first try at x 774.8 failed
    that check (23 $ against a 6 $ cap); x 806 clears it.
  - **Shape: a `prof`, not the usual wedge.** H 2.6, profile
    `[-6.7, 0, -5.2, 0.255, -3.6, 0.8, 0, 2.6, 1.0, 2.6, 16, 0]`, 16 m wide
    (the road is 13.6 m) with a 2.5 m skirt each side.
    - The foot is curved in three steps (slopes 0.17, 0.34, 0.50). A plain
      wedge this steep (tried: H 2.6, run 5) is an 8.7 m/s jolt at its foot at
      60 km/h; cars.js reads it as a landing (impact 0.64, 1.5 damage) and
      main.js fires the *crash* slow-mo, whose 2.5 s cooldown then blocks the
      *jump* slow-mo in the air. Seen in the harness: 1.88 s of air, slow-mo 0,
      `cool 0.33` at 1.6 s of air. With the curved foot: impact 0.22 to 0.25,
      0 damage on the way up, and the jump slow-mo fires.
    - Two-way road, so no cliffs: a metre of flat top, then a 15 m west face.
      An eastbound truck on the way home rides up it and hops off the steep
      side (1.07 s, no speed lost, 0.8 damage), which is when the box on the
      bench falls.
  - `noTrack: true` (the road is the run-up); `entry` and `from` (harness
    run-up points) are on chemin d'Aylmer east of Fraser.
  - buildFeatures emits a `prof` feature when a jump has `prof`; every other
    ramp is byte-identical (still a `pad` with `[0, 0, run, 0]`).
- **The callout is timed to it** (src/game/missions.js). `RAMP_CALL_D` 140 m to
  90 m (exported), so « Essaie le tremplin » lands as you come down Fraser to

## Found, outside this task's files (for the lead)

- **Ramps are not drawn on index.html.** main.js bakes the world in a Worker
  (`buildSectors(..., { worker: true })`), and src/game/world/sector-worker.js
  never calls `installJumps()`, so the worker's FEATURES have no jumps and no
  ramp mesh is baked. The main thread's physics does have them
  (restoreWorldQueries builds its terrain after main.js installed them). In the
  B-04 stills the road at the lip is flat asphalt and the truck flies off
  nothing. This is every ramp in the table, not just garnotte. A likely fix is
  one line in sector-worker.js `bake()`: `(await import('../jumps.js')).installJumps();`
  before `bakeWorld`. Even then, heightfield.js paints any road crossing a
  feature's triangles onto it, so an on-road ramp reads as raised asphalt;
  a `noRoadPaint` opt-out would let the garnotte read as gravel.
- The harness `jumps` beat stops measuring 40 m past the lip, so a fast ramp
  reads short (garnotte 1.31 s at 84.5 km/h there, 2.42 s when flown to the
  ground in the probe).
