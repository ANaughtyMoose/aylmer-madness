# A-02 Damage and handbrake tuning

## What changed (src/game/cars.js)
- Damage per impact is now `knockDamage(closing)`: 34 x (min(1.6, closing/18) - 0.08)^1.5, and nothing under 3 km/h closing (no damage, no camera impact, no rumble). A flat 25 coefficient on the old capped force cannot reach the plan's own ">= 45 at 90 km/h" bar (its ceiling is under 25), so the curve is steeper at the top and much cheaper at the bottom.
- Hydro poles keep their six-hit balance: `POLE_SCALE` makes a full-knock pole hit cost 14.8, as before.
- Ranger handbrake: hbGrip 0.72 to 0.45, hbYaw 1.06 to 1.5, plus a new per-car `hbSwing` (2.7, Ranger only). Changing the table alone could not give a 180: the yaw in this model comes from vLong, which runs out once the nose passes 90 degrees, so every car (Civic included) stalled near 110 degrees on a held lever. `hbSwing` adds yaw momentum into the existing `yawSpin` while the lever is up, scaled by hbYaw (so the Posi upgrade still adds swing).
