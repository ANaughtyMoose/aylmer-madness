# B-01a Never stranded: golf night ends and Y tow

Plan v2, wave B, shift B1. Branch rf/b-01a from fix/look-on-qr 9d19d9e.

## What changed

- `src/game/setpieces/golfnuit.js`: `tearDown()` no longer calls `swapBack()` with the 25 m `near` limit. New `backToCameIn(G, m)` puts the player back in the vehicle they came in (`m.came`, set by the first stage; `m.truck` or the own vehicle as fallbacks; never the cart) whatever the distance, through `cartkit.swapTo`. Every end runs it, because every end (done, fail, restart, quit, abandon) goes through main.js `closeMission` then `missionCleanup` then `def.cleanup`. A lapsed retry window changes nothing, since cleanup has already run. The bike goes back where it was before the night (`m.bikeHome`) and the cart goes back to its apron. A 0.7 s fade from black (a DOM div, browser only) hides the swap. The narrator line (French) is « Tu reprends ton Ranger au stationnement du Club. ». After a success it is « Le lendemain matin, tu reprends ton Ranger au stationnement du Club. », because the fence card already says the truck waits until the morning. If you came in another car the line is « Tu reprends ton char au stationnement du Club. », and on a bike « Tu reprends ton vélo au premier tertre. ».
- Same file, the retry path. `failMission` puts you in the Ranger now, so a free retry that resumes a cart stage would have found you in the truck. New `ensureRig(G, m, need, spot)` runs from the keys, sayyad, joyride, chase and fence stages. It does nothing on a normal run (`m.came` is set). On a resumed job it puts you back on the bike, then in the cart at the flag before. It also brings back the owners and the lights if the chase is resumed after its first flag.
- `src/game/setpieces/cartkit.js`: new `swapTo(G, id, opts)`, which swaps into a parked vehicle at any distance. `swapBack` is unchanged (royal.js still uses it with its own `near`).
- `src/game/tow.js`: new `ownVehicleId`, `isBorrowed`, `jobVehicle`, `ownVehicleStatus` and `returnToOwnVehicle`. `towLine` has a new swapped wording. In a borrowed vehicle (the cart, a bike, a friend's loaner), Y brings YOUR vehicle on the flatbed to the nearest clear road spot beside you. It comes repaired, at the normal flatbed price (`chargeTow`: free once a day, on the slate when broke). The borrowed vehicle goes to its `homeParked()` spot. In your own vehicle, or in a vehicle a live job put you in, it is exactly `callTow(G)`. tow.js now imports `characterById` and `homeParked` from save.js, and `CARS` from cars.js. There is no import cycle.
- `tools/smoke_cart.mjs`: new group 6 (the 15 ends, the retry resume, came-on-a-bike).
- `tools/smoke_tow.mjs`: new group 7 (Y from the cart, a bike and the Civic loaner, plus the edge cases).
- `tools/review/beats/B-01a.mjs` (new): a script beat in the real game. Golf night, then abandon 300 m out, must land you in the Ranger at the lot. Then Y from the cart 1 km away, through `returnToOwnVehicle`, must land you in the Ranger next to you.
- Nothing is ever placed at 299 Fraser. The fallback spot for a lost Ranger is the Club lot, so nose-in is never in play. smoke_homeparking is green.

## Contract notes (for B-12a, main.js; B-06, hud.js)

1. **Y handler** (`doTow`, main.js about line 1435): replace `const r = tow.callTow(G);` with `const r = tow.returnToOwnVehicle(G, 'tow');`. Keep the rest. `tow.towLine(r)` already words the swapped case, and `r.ok === false` still means no road spot. In your own vehicle, or in a vehicle the live job put you in (`G.mission.cameIn`, `G.mission.truck`, or `def.keepVehicle`), it falls through to `callTow` unchanged. A Y in the middle of the golf night still tows the cart and does not end the job.
2. **Optional ownership hook**: by default a car is borrowed if it is the cart, a bike, or a car whose `who` is not 'Yours' or 'Le lot'. If the garage knows better (for example the Saturn becomes Tom's), set `G.ownsCar = (id) => garage.has(id, G.done) && ...` once. `G.ownCarId` overrides the own vehicle id. Neither hook is needed for the acceptance.
3. **swapCar** (main.js line 1511): no change is needed for B-01a. golfnuit calls `G.swapCar` as before. If B-12a wants the plan's "returnToOwnVehicle in swapCar", the only safe use is in the garage hand-back, which is B-01b's `ensureOwnVehicle`. Do not call it inside `swapCar` itself, because golfnuit's own swaps go through it.
4. **HUD marker** (B-06, hud.js): `tow.ownVehicleStatus(G)` returns `{ id, current, inOwn, borrowed, jobVehicle, at: {x, z, yaw} | null, dist }`. Show the own-truck marker at `at` when `!inOwn`, and the wrong-vehicle chip when `borrowed && !jobVehicle`.
