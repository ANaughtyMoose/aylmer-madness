## What changed

- **Season by date (peds.js).** `inSeason(rec, day)` reads the summer calendar (calendar.js `G.day`). A line naming another season (snow, Halloween, the rentrée, sugar shack and so on: `OFF_SEASON`) is never said. A line naming a date is only said around it: the Saint-Jean scratch tickets 1 June to 1 July, Canada Day 25 June to 2 July, Labour Day from 25 August. A record in ambient.json may carry its own `from` / `to` (month-day); applyAmbient keeps them. With no calendar (prototype, node suites) a dated line stays quiet. The filter rides on the existing skip predicate, so an exhausted zone still borrows or stays silent instead of looping. Today it drops 1 of 118 lines on 22 July (the Saint-Jean tickets).
- **Traffic on the main drags (traffic.js).** Respawns into the 120-300 m ring are a weighted reservoir: residential edges at 0.3 against 1 for tertiary and up. Opening spawn share on the bigger roads 0.6 to 0.75. Held at Principale for ten minutes: 45 % of respawns land on the bigger roads where the ring offers 22 % by edge count; 35 vehicles inside 420 m (26 cars, 7 bikes, 2 buses).
- **Near-miss events for C-01 (traffic.js).** See the contract below. Nothing scores them yet.

## Contract note for C-01 (and whoever owns main.js)

- `G.traffic.nearMisses`: this frame's events, cleared at the top of `update()`. Each is `{ t, gap, speed, kind, car }`: `gap` metres of air between the two boxes' sides, `speed` closing speed in m/s, `kind` 'car' | 'bike' | 'city' | 'school'.
- `G.traffic.onNearMiss = (ev) => ...`: optional callback, called as each happens.
- `G.traffic.drainNearMisses()`: everything since the last drain, oldest first, at most 32.
- `G.traffic.nearCount`: running total.
- Rules: closing speed over 30 km/h, boxes abreast, gap 0.05 to 1.5 m, one per car per 3 s. Any contact with the player arms the same 3 s cooldown, so a touch never counts; a stunned car never counts. Wiring is main.js work (not owned by A-09).
