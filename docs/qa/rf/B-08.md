# B-08 Hush the street during scripted scenes; cut the chore lines

Branch rf/b-08 (plan v2 names it rf/b08-hush; the lead's dispatch said rf/b-08), from fix/look-on-qr at 36dc188. Shift B2.

## What changed

- **src/game/heckle.js: the scene hush.** Two ways to hold it: `hush(reason, secs)` (a time-to-live, refreshed every tick) and `hushWhile(reason, test, maxSecs = HUSH_MAX)` (held while `test()` is true, at most 60 s). New export `toastLive(hud, text)` says whether a toast is still queued or on screen (reads `hud.toastLive()` if the HUD ever grows one, else the ToastQueue's `active` and `pending`). Why the second way: the first harness run (pack `references/review/rf-b08-selfcheck`) showed the four antipoison toasts come up 40 s after the call stage, behind a backlog in the one-slot toast queue, with « PIÉTON / Y conduit bien, lui. » between them. So every nuittim scene toast now holds the street until it has left the screen. Details: New `hush(reason, secs = HUSH_TTL)`, `unhush(reason?)`, getter `hushed`, `hushReasons()` and counter `hushDropped`. While any reason is live, `say()` (every heckle trigger, the clean-driving praise included) and `ambient()` (peds.js street chatter, hangout.js next-yard lines, any speakerless `line()`) return null. Story lines (`line(who, ...)`) still queue and play. Raising the hush takes any heckle or street bubble already on screen down. Each raise lasts `HUSH_TTL` = 1.0 s of game time, so a scene refreshes it every tick, and a scene that stops ticking (fail, Backspace, teleport) frees the street within a second by itself. `clear()` (the menu) and `reset()` (new game) lift every reason.
- **src/game/setpieces/nuittim.js** routes every scene toast (the call, the watch lines and the E checks, the stranger, the walk, the gars de l'école) through `toastScene()`, which holds `hushWhile('nuittim:' + text, ...)` until that toast is gone. It also raises `hush('nuittim')` every tick of the six scene stages: the Centre antipoison call, the watch, the stranger and the black cut, the dazed walk, the gars de l'école, the last metres home. The two drive legs (to Mike's, to the Tim) keep the town. `cleanup()` calls `unhush('nuittim')`. Exported `HUSH = 'nuittim'`.
- **src/game/arc.js (hush flag only):** the finale raises `hush('finale')` every tick of the phone and kitchen-table stages, and holds the street for as long as each callback toast in the free drive is queued or showing (`sceneToast()`). The Saint-Jean fire (`francais` stage of arcbache, same file) raises `hush('stjean')` every tick and holds it for its opening scene toast, as the plan's What asks. No other line of arc.js changed.
- **assets/text/ambient.json:** 16 chore lines cut, 1 rewritten (the Saint-Jean scratch-ticket errand is now something the dep guy said, so smoke_street_life's dated line survives). 118 to 102 overheard lines (smoke_chatter needs at least 100). « Rentrez les vélos dans remise » stays on purpose: hangout.js plays the residential dusk lines as the next yard over, where somebody being told to put the bikes away is the point, and smoke_hangout needs four of them. The `police` block is byte-identical (60 lines, sha1 of the block unchanged).
- **src/game/peds.js:** the fallback pool's « Ferme le boyau d'arrosage » is replaced by an existing ambient.json line (the garbage truck); a header note says the street carries no chores.
- **assets/text/heckles.json:** 19 lines lose a trailing or inline sacre (French and English gloss both), nothing else changed. **src/game/heckle.js** built-in pool: « Ostie que t'es dangereux! » becomes « Mon doux que t'es dangereux! », « Ralentis, mon tabarnak! » becomes « Ralentis, coudonc! » (glosses updated, the old gloss's dash gone).
- **tools/smoke_heckle_queue.mjs:** four new groups (the hush API; 1,000 simulated seconds of the Nuit du Tim; swearing per pool and overall; chore lines and the police count).
