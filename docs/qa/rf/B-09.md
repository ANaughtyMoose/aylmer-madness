# B-09 Trauma shake and capped hitstop on an event ladder

Branch `rf/b-09`, from fix/look-on-qr at 9d19d9e. Shift B1, merge 7 of 8 (before B-12a).

## What changed

- **src/game/feel.js (new).** A pure module (no imports, no DOM, no G, no clock of its own). `createFeel({ sink })` returns one feel state:
  - Trauma in 0..1, added per event from the ladder, falling back linearly at 1.2 per second. Shake = trauma squared on 2D simplex noise (one channel per axis, 13 noise units per second), so it never repeats and never beats against the frame rate.
  - The ladder (`LADDER`): gravel 0.10, near miss 0.15, mailbox 0.25, bin 0.25, cop ram 0.50, head-on 0.80. Gravel and near miss are rate limited (0.4 s and 0.3 s per kind) so a surface that reports every frame cannot pin the shake.
  - Hitstop by importance: mailbox and bin 40 ms, cop ram 70 ms, head-on 100 ms; gravel and near miss none. At most one per 0.5 s (from its start), never two at once, and skipped when the caller says the A-06 slow-mo is running.
  - `cameraOffset(scale)`: x, y, z in metres (vector clamped to 0.35 m) and pitch, yaw, roll in radians (each clamped to 2.5 degrees). `scale` is clamped to 0..1 so the shake setting can never break the bound. The returned object is reused (no allocation per frame).
  - `kindForImpact(impact, { cop })` maps `v.impact` (0..1) to a rung: >= 0.6 head-on, >= 0.35 (or a cop car from 0.15) cop ram, >= 0.12 bin, else nothing.
  - Telemetry: with `sink` set, `sink.offset` (largest offset length in metres since `resetTelemetry`) and `sink.hitstops` (count) are written as they change. `telemetry()` also gives the largest angle, skips, events and the last 16 hitstops.
- **tools/smoke_feel.mjs.** The existing vehicle-feel smoke is kept as it was; a new section « B-09 feel.js » is added before the report (29 checks). The file now has 61 checks.
- **tools/review/beats/B-09.mjs (new).** Script beat. Part 1 (In-task): imports feel.js in the real page and checks a head-on gives a 40 to 100 ms hitstop, a shake over 0.05 m, and that a second hit inside 0.5 s gives none. Part 2 (After integration): once `AYLMER.feel()` carries `offset` and `hitstops`, aims the truck off the road at Principale as the crash beat does, floors it, and asserts top speed >= 40 km/h, offset > 0.05 m, and hitstops between 1 and the crash slow-mo count. Before wiring it returns `wired: false` and asserts nothing.

Nothing is placed in the world and no game-visible text is added, so public-place and the cast-line rule do not apply; smoke_lang and smoke_story_rules pass inside `npm test`.

## Per-frame cost

0.00015 ms per frame against the 0.05 ms cap (about 0.3% of it). One `update`, one `cameraOffset` (six simplex samples while trauma > 0, none when it is 0) and one `timeScale`; no allocation per frame.

## CONTRACT NOTES (for B-12a, src/main.js)

In `AYLMER.step` (about 3553) call `G.feelFx.update(dt)` next to `slowmoScale(dt)`; whether step() skips its tick during a hitstop is the owner's call (the harness counts telemetry either way).
