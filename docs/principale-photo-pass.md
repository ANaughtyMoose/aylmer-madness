# Principale photo pass — Tom's 2004 recollections

The 2007 views are the period baseline. Later photographs supply building detail, not later tenants. The Sol logo is an approximation based on Tom's supplied green-and-gold sun sign; the store is clean, in the left half of the PFK building at 195 Principale. Sayyad and Zahra love it.

Tom confirmed the grey stone building beside the recreation complex is the old library, closed for water damage. The redbrick clock-tower building is a separate site. He also confirmed the former Moca-Loca is the redbrick house with the green roof and porch.

Street View labels indicate camera addresses, so the reference-to-parcel matches use building shapes and map tenants. The garage door and map identify 143 Principale (Garage Hugo Caumartin), although the camera reads 146. The pizza/gelato reference labelled 153 is matched to the redbrick former-house restaurant on the opposite parcel (map 152); the cafe with the green roof is matched to map 150. Dr Morin's pictured grey clinic is matched to the dental parcel at map 48, across from the 47/49 camera positions. These are visual parcel matches, not independent confirmation of historical civic numbers.
