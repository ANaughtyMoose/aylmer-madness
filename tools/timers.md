# Story clocks vs. the map

Generated by `node tools/timers.mjs` (`--pace` re-drives the pace car). The rule: a clock is
(pace + 3 s to read the prompt) x 1.35, rounded to 5 s. Road pace is the race.js pace car in the Ranger from a
standstill on the real bake (tools/timers_pace.json); cart pace is 29 km/h straight
flag to flag plus 4 s per stop-and-E. `route m` is the GPS route (road) or the straight
line (cart). `ceil s` is the least a clock may give (the per-class ceiling on what it asks);
`floor s` the most a clock may give before it stops mattering. tools/smoke_timers.mjs
fails the build when a clock is outside [ceil, floor].

## Notes

- A stage with no `time` is untimed and is not listed. The golf night and Russell's chase carry
  one clock over several stages (`m.chaseLeft`): one row each, legs summed.
- The getaway at Sayyad's passes at 300 m in a straight line (`anywhere`): the pace car is
  stopped there, not at the marker.
- Route length is a lower bound: no U-turns, no overshoot, no traffic. The pace car meets
  traffic-free roads; the 1.35 is the human's and the traffic's share.
- `nearestRoad` in the static table is a stand-in for world.js's, so a place may snap a metre
  or two differently from the pace run.
