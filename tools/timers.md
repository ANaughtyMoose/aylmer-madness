## Notes

- Stages with no `time` inherit the previous stage's clock (`applyStage` only
  resets `timeLeft` when the stage declares one), so a `—` row is untimed.
- A **total** row only shows an implied average when every stage is timed.
- `tour` is one clock per checkpoint and `dep` is the other tight one — those
  are the rows to look at first.
- Route length is a lower bound on what you actually drive: no U-turns, no
  overshooting the stage radius, no waiting for traffic.
- `nearestRoad` here is a stand-in for `world.js`'s (same rule: nearest point
  on a non-service road), so a place may snap a metre or two differently.
